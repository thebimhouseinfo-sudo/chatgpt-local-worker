# Skill — Scope & Constraints

Turn the request into a planning contract before proposing implementation.

Capture:
- observable objective;
- explicit user/repository constraints;
- compatibility/platform requirements;
- non-goals and adjacent cleanup excluded from this plan;
- assumptions supported by evidence;
- unresolved decisions that require user/product input.

Do not hide ambiguity by choosing a product behavior on behalf of the user. When multiple valid implementations depend on an unresolved decision, describe the decision and its consequence instead of pretending one path is mandatory.
